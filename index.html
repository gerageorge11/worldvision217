WorldVision Naples 217

From the companion app of the WorldVision Song Contest

The WorldVision Naples 217 is the upcoming 217th edition of WorldVision Song Contest, hosted by Radiotelevisione italiana (RAI), which will stage the event on behalf of the Public Broadcasting Company of Ukraine (UA:PBC), which had won the 216th contest for Ukraine with the song "Kryla Leleky" by Nikita Kiselyov but was unable to stage the event due to time and budget constraints after hosting the 216th edition in Uzhhorod, Transcarpathian Region of Ukraine.

Location

The 217th contest will take place in Naples, Italy. It will be the thirteenth time that Italy has hosted the contest, having previously done so in WorldVision 13, 14, 23, 38, 40, 60, 79, 106, 123, 144, 186 and 197. For the current Italian administration, it will be their third hosting, after delivering spectacular shows in Avellino 186 and Naples 197. The selected venue for the contest is yet to be announced.

Bidding phase

Following Ukraine’s victory in WorldVision 216, discussions over the hosting of the next edition began immediately. Although UA:PBC initially explored the possibility of staging a second consecutive contest in Ukraine, broadcaster officials soon indicated that the compressed preparation window and the financial burden of back-to-back editions made that scenario impractical.

RAI subsequently entered negotiations with UA:PBC and the WorldVision Reference Group, offering to stage the contest in Italy on Ukraine’s behalf. The proposal was welcomed for pairing Italy’s production experience with a concept capable of preserving the celebratory spirit of the Ukrainian win.

A domestic bidding phase was then opened in Italy, with several cities informally linked to the event. Naples quickly emerged as a frontrunner thanks to its rich event-hosting pedigree, strong transport links, and its evocative Mediterranean atmosphere, which aligned neatly with the eventual Matrimonio theme. Other cities reportedly explored in internal discussions included Rome, Turin, Bologna and Bari.

By mid-summer, Naples had become the preferred host city, especially given its successful staging of WorldVision 197 and its reputation for colourful, emotionally resonant productions. While the host city was subsequently confirmed, the specific venue for the contest remained under evaluation at the time of announcement, with organisers signalling that further production details would follow in due course.
